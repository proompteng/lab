# Recent strategy research for Bayn

Research cut: October 6, 2026, America/Los_Angeles. This is a research decision record, not a strategy qualification or an activation request.

## Decision

The tested 15-minute trend adaptation does not justify deployment: every SPY/QQQ policy and timing combination lost money at an additional 1 bp per side over January–September 2026. The public news-nowcasting returns also fail to establish a risk-matched selection advantage. Remove both from the list of presumed winners. Keep Jev and the existing deterministic policies as comparison baselines; their presence in the repository does not establish an edge.

The immediate work is a complete comparison on original retained inputs with independent portfolios and attributable costs. A cheap cost-aware model is a subsequent comparator, not an established replacement. September's MFAST paper deserves a replication audit for a separate daily news track. Its reported results cannot be transferred to Bayn's one-position, long-only, 15-minute contract. No candidate reviewed here establishes a profitable Bayn replacement.

## Candidate definitions and audit decisions

### Intraday trend plus fast entry information

[Zarattini and Pagani's February 2026 note](https://concretumgroup.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Improving-Performance-with-Fast-Alphas-A-Tactical-Overlay-for-Intraday-Trend-Trading.pdf) reports improved SPY trend performance from a reversal overlay that loses after commissions on its own. Its simulation assumes no slippage and also delays stop exits without a maximum waiting time.

The paired adaptation has now been tested retrospectively with frozen rules and failed the declared cost scenarios. Preserve that negative result rather than tuning its parameters on the same outcomes. Protective exits remained immediate. The test does not refute the original paper's different holding, direction, or sizing rules.

The reference baseline uses bands at the session open plus or minus half of ATR over 14 periods, checks breakouts every 15 minutes, and can hold to session close. The entry overlay waits for a five-minute move against the intended direction. A 15-minute maximum holding time, long-only execution, bounded sizing, or a different price feed changes the experiment. Freeze those differences explicitly. Supply genuine prior-session prices and volume rather than manufacturing them from the current rolling window.

### Daily long-only news selection

[Chen and Pu's January 2026 paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.11958v1) collects overnight scores before outcomes and reports Sharpe 2.43 for the top 20 Russell 1000 stocks. Returns run from one opening auction to the next. Its spread estimates are not complete AI, data, impact, and operating-cost accounting. The [authors' public results file](https://github.com/mapledust0/AI-Stock-Nowcasting/blob/main/data.json), read on this research cut, has 214 dates from April 15, 2025, through February 20, 2026.

The newly calculated risk and cost diagnostics below weaken the headline return comparison. This remains an information-signal lead requiring an overnight portfolio, a broad universe, and archived source retrieval and scores. Bayn's current 15-symbol, one-position, intraday Jev runtime cannot replicate it. An independently timestamped research portfolio must compare against passive and deterministic selection. Re-running today's model over historical headlines cannot recreate the original forecasts.

### Published news returns: risk, selection and costs

The authors' aggregate file was fetched again and pinned to SHA-256 `cc9f82da557aeb48404bb4b383abf81cef291a1691c2c2b8a9675425e3ac8dad`. It has 214 observations and 39 portfolio sizes. Compounding the top-20 series gives 54.01%, versus 25.97% for the supplied benchmark, but annualized sample volatility is 21.65% versus 11.83%. Constant full-sample volatility matching reduces invested exposure to 54.63% and compound return to 27.25%. This scaling is descriptive and uses future observations; it is not an executable ex-ante allocation. Cash return and risk-free rate are set to zero.

Equal-volatility mean excess return is 0.4735 bp daily. Centered circular block resampling used 10,000 draws at each of 5, 10 and 20 sessions, common date draws across all 39 sizes, and fixed observed standard deviations. Every conditional 95% mean interval includes zero. Family-adjusted one-sided max-statistic probabilities for top-20 are 0.709, 0.748 and 0.805. This is a specified conditional diagnostic, not a full White reality check, SPA or deflated-Sharpe implementation. Unreported prompts, models and research choices are outside its correction.

Turnover and operating invoices are not observed. Under the explicit scenario of replacing the entire invested stock book daily, an additional 0.5 bp per side lowers the matched compound return to 25.77%, below the uncharged benchmark's 25.97%. One bp lowers it to 24.31%. These are sensitivity scenarios, not reconstructed actual execution costs. Aggregate returns alone cannot verify timestamped forecasts, tradable fills or a profitable Bayn strategy.

### Trend adaptation: January–September 2026

The supplementary experiment uses raw historical IEX five-minute SPY and QQQ bars, 187 exchange sessions and 374 symbol-sessions. All 29,172 expected regular-session bars are present. Single-symbol monthly requests have no remaining page token. Daily observations starting in November 2025 provide strictly prior Wilder ATR. Source bytes, requests, simulator and frozen protocols have retained hashes.

Each independent portfolio starts at $10,000, holds whole shares in at most one position, caps entry exposure at 20%, holds for at most 15 minutes, retains a 50 bp protective stop, and flattens five minutes before close. The paired policies use an open-plus-half-ATR breakout with and without a negative five-minute entry pullback. Decisions use completed bars and fills use the next eligible bar open, with either zero or one additional five-minute bar of lag. Intrabar stop timestamps and original publication times are unknown. SPY is a paper benchmark diagnostic and is excluded from native Jev's candidate universe.

## Additional August–September 2026 evidence

[MFAST, September 20](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.23703v1), tests news-driven daily portfolios from June 2024 through January 2026. It reports long Sharpe 1.72 and long-short 2.85 at 5 bp one-way costs with a 10% daily dollar-volume participation cap; its public-data arm reports Sharpe 0.92. The reported LLaMA-3 inference footprint is 54.2 GB and 112 ms per article, versus FinBERT's 4.4 GB and 8.5 ms. These are author measurements on their hardware. The paper describes a replication package, but a downloadable package was not located in the inspected links or targeted searches. This is a separate daily replication lead, not evidence for native intraday profit.

[HERALD, August 19](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10489-026-07407-7), combines news with stock relations. Its test folds end in 2024 and licensed raw headlines are unavailable here. The inspected training target is close-to-close while the simulated trade is next-open-to-close. That creates a target/realizable-horizon mismatch to audit; it is not by itself proof of future leakage. Cost-adjusted independent replication is unverified.

[VST, September 7](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.07065v1), evaluates an agentic alpha-search run on CSI 1000. Its full factor-pool median Sharpe is 0.13, compared with 0.34 for the first 500 factors. Selected top-20 portfolio median Sharpe is 0.71 gross, while the all-portfolio median is negative. Single-run selection evidence and gross returns do not establish transferable net US intraday alpha.

[The September 28 PEP/KO case study](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.35359v1) searches 901 policies. Removing the COVID window lowers selected Sharpe to 0.28; deflated-Sharpe probability is 0.423. Its frozen 2023–present test reports one trade and -$28,131; the one-trade Sharpe is not reliable. A supposedly familiar pair still needs current relationship and execution validation.

[Switching-cost regularization, September 22](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10614-026-11437-1), reduces signal changes through previous-prediction feedback. Its four FX series use training through Q1 2025 and testing in Q2 2025. This motivates a small turnover-aware supervised-model comparison, not a new reinforcement-learning system or proof of equity profits.

[Concentrated-market allocation, September 22](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.27113v1), evaluates monthly stock portfolios through 2024 with 15 bp proportional costs. It belongs to allocation research rather than the native 15-minute strategy. New clustering, volatility-gating and memory-augmented reinforcement-learning papers below remain abstract-only leads. The two NBER papers concern evaluation and asset-management outcomes rather than a specified executable intraday policy.

### Cost-aware daily statistical arbitrage

[Epstein, Wang, Choi, and Pelger's 2025 paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.11616v1) jointly learns factors and allocations with trading costs in the objective. It reports net Sharpe around 2.3. The daily test spans January 1998 through December 2021. Costs are 5 bp on weight changes plus 1 bp on short exposure. This does not establish current borrow availability, impact, or recent profitability.

Keep this as a separate research track. It needs roughly 500 equities, characteristics, training, short positions, and daily portfolio allocation. It is a poor first deployment for the current native intraday service. Test the published construction on recent untouched data before adding that operational footprint.

### Cost-aware short-horizon models

[The October 1, 2026 Frontiers study](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/applied-mathematics-and-statistics/articles/10.3389/fams.2026.1888767/full) compares trees, Random Forest, and XGBoost at one to 30 minutes. Its data end in December 2023. Thirty minutes usually performs better; only some assets remain profitable. Costs are fixed assumptions, not observed execution spreads. Session handling and the selection procedure need review before replication.

Use its cost-aware target construction and break-even slippage diagnostic. A shallow model is a reasonable cheap comparator. Do not deploy an asset/model/horizon selected from the paper's winning test results as if it were already independently validated. Its preferred horizon also exceeds Bayn's present maximum hold.

## Recent evidence that changes the priority

The [September 2025 blue-chip study](https://www.businessperspectives.org/index.php/component/zoo/high-frequency-momentum-and-contrarian-strategies-in-u-s-blue-chips) tests 288 momentum and contrarian variants on 2020–2021 tick data. Its abstract says none outperform the benchmark after reasonable transaction costs. This weakens the case for deploying generic fast price patterns because they are already coded.

The [September 2026 ORB working paper](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=7428398) reports a preregistered 225-cell futures study and negative cost conclusions. Full-text retrieval failed here. Futures ORB is not the same hypothesis as stock selection using abnormal opening volume. It is relevant counterevidence, not a universal refutation of all ORB strategies.

The [2024 stocks-in-play paper](https://www.alexandria.unisg.ch/server/api/core/bitstreams/3c2989c4-688d-4d78-8a71-f02690990d51/content) reports Sharpe 2.81 after commissions for a top-20 opening-volume portfolio over 2016–2023. It uses both directions and permits 4x leverage. Generic breakout over Bayn's fixed universe does not reproduce its unusual-volume selection or economics. Keep this older recent paper as a reference definition, not today's strongest proof.

[Chen and Welch's July 2026 paper, with an August draft date](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.06502v1), examines about 200 published equity anomalies. Combining a post-2005 sample and an investable-stock filter reduces median gross long-short return to about 7 bp monthly. Its scope is those published anomaly implementations. It does not rule out every dynamic or information-driven strategy.

The [October 2025 news-conditioned momentum paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.26228v1) reports an out-of-sample Sharpe improvement from 0.79 to 1.06 for January 2024–March 2025 under 2 bp trading costs. Monthly rebalancing works better than weekly. This supports examining news as a slower selection overlay, rather than assuming that frequent model decisions earn their costs.

The [April 2026 multi-agent recommendations paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.17327v1) reports positive excess performance for its S&P 100 cohort but a p-value of 0.17. That cohort does not establish a statistically persuasive selection edge. The presence of multiple agents is not evidence of profitable trading.

The [May 2026 Strat-LLM paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.06024v1) describes a live 2025 evaluation and includes GPT-5.4 in its model comparison. [OpenAI dates GPT-5.4's release to March 5, 2026](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-5-4/). That chronology needs an explicit explanation of which comparisons were retrospective. No transaction-cost specification was found in the inspected HTML. Do not treat its comparisons as established prospective net-profit evidence.

## Broader recent checks

The [New York Fed's July 1, 2026 update](https://libertystreeteconomics.newyorkfed.org/2026/07/the-disappearing-overnight-drift/) extends the old overnight-drift analysis through December 2025. The 2–3 a.m. Eastern futures window averages close to zero in 2021–2025. Its closing-imbalance relationship also weakens. This is direct recent counterevidence to recycling an older overnight anomaly without a new test.

[Aleti, Bollerslev, and Siggaard's 2025 journal paper](https://public.econ.duke.edu/~boller/Papers/MS_2025.pdf) studies 15-minute ETF timing from high-frequency cross-sectional factors. Its out-of-sample period is 2004–2020. It uses observed TAQ spreads and fixed short-borrow assumptions. The methods deserve a recent-data replication, but the publication year does not make its performance contemporary. Bayn's small-universe rolling-window features cannot reproduce the full factor panel.

The [2026 BTC forecasting preprint](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.00060v1) tests 27 walk-forward folds through 2025. Filtering weak forecasts relative to a 10 bp assumed turnover cost cuts trading sharply. Its flagship long-only XGBoost result reports 65.4% annualized return, Sharpe 1.09, and about 67.4% maximum drawdown. Code is available on request, not independently reproduced here. The result motivates testing a cost threshold, not importing a crypto return target or accepting that drawdown.

The [September 2026 AlphaZeroBeta article](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s40854-026-00955-4) reports daily market-neutral reinforcement-learning portfolios over 2014–2024. It charges fixed spread and regional borrow rates. Some input vintages and its next-day-close convention still need independent replication. The authors identify a fixed 2025 constituent snapshot for one Chinese universe as a survivorship caveat. Its broad fundamentals, macro, news, and options inputs require a separate portfolio track.

The [August 2026 multi-level market-making paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.18195v1) trains and evaluates in a simulated order-book environment. It demonstrates an allocation method, not observed live net profit. Queue position, fills, adverse selection, venue access, and costs would need their own evidence. Bayn's current quote-bound IOC path cannot inherit a market maker's simulated fill economics.

The [2026 order-flow study's author repository](https://github.com/kethanse66-cyber/quant-research-ofi) covers April 2024–April 2025 and explicitly concludes that its strongest ten-second signal does not survive costs. The associated SSRN full text was unavailable. A [February 2026 NSE order-imbalance study](https://www.ubplj.org/index.php/jpm/article/view/2235) also reports disappearing abnormal returns after costs; its full text requires paid access and was not purchased. Neither result rules out all slower order-flow uses.

[Mesfin's MNQ falsification preprint, revised September 15, 2026](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.04004), uses data ending in August 2025. Fourteen common families fail its joint criteria. The latest version also reports two positive controls, so an all-signals-fail paraphrase would be wrong. The RTH control has declared contamination; the London control changes sign with a 15-minute delay and assumes exact stop fills. The paper leaves bar-boundary versus accessible-timing explanations unresolved. These controls need tick-level replication before adoption.

The [October 2026 volatility-forecasting article's publisher abstract](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1544612326011001) reports that none of more than 30 language models significantly beats log-HAR. Reported portfolio gains are attributed to a covariance-induced equity tilt, vanish at 15 bp costs, and concentrate in one half-year. Full-text access failed here. This weakens an expensive language-model volatility component as a first choice; a cheap volatility benchmark must be included.

Two new methodology papers affect every candidate. [The May 2026 decision-time benchmark](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.23959v1) isolates protocol violations under shared data and portfolios. Same-day-open fills using later daily-bar fields and centered features generate large inflation. [The March 2026 engine study](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.20319v1) finds cost-driven differences across implementations. Its text describes one maximum divergence as both return and Sharpe divergence in different sections, so that headline is not used here. Bayn should reconcile individual fills and fees, then independently recalculate economic totals; a second engine's metric is not automatically the truth.

The [July 2026 industry-adjusted reversal article](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0165176526003095) reports 0.53% monthly and Sharpe 0.74 across 64 countries, with improvement over ordinary reversal after assumed costs. Full-text access failed, so its data endpoint, shorting assumptions, and net result remain unaudited here. This is a worthwhile slower portfolio lead, not evidence for a 60-second residual rebound. The industry panel and monthly evaluation require their own definition.

The [August 2026 U.S. pairs preprint](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=7204658) reports GOOG/GOOGL net Sharpe 1.38 over 20 walk-forward folds. Its abstract also reports 0.82 after selection adjustment, a 15.4 bp round-trip break-even cost, and 0.48–1.38 across fill conventions. Most reversion arrives overnight; no other pair clears its significance threshold. Full methods were unavailable. Test this structural pair on untouched recent data and actual borrow/fills before generalizing to a pairs engine.

The [August 2026 ETF pairs study](https://zenodo.org/records/22059837), whose downloaded PDF is dated August 29, uses adjusted closes through August 21, 2026, with selection through 2018. It fits 504 days and trades the next 126 at 2 bp one-way on gross notional. Four accepted discoveries have Sharpe estimates indistinguishable from zero. These optimistic fixed costs do not establish current borrow and fill economics. Selected full-text methods were inspected after the web PDF fetch failed. This counterexample makes generic daily cointegration screening a low priority; correlation cannot substitute for executable spread economics.

[Dew-Becker and Giglio's September 2025 Chicago Fed paper](https://www.chicagofed.org/-/media/publications/working-papers/2025/wp2025-17.pdf?sc_lang=en) studies traded options through 2022 and finds their CAPM alpha converges toward zero after about 2010. It distinguishes synthetic hedges from traded options and explicitly says historical synthesis could have been infeasible after frictions. This is recent publication with older observations. Option selling requires a current cost, margin, and tail-risk study; the old variance-premium narrative alone does not justify adding it to Bayn.

## What this changes in the deployment work

The comparison capability has been delivered separately; complete original-session input and the full native comparison remain outstanding. Keep the four already implemented policies as baselines. Retain the failed trend diagnostic as research evidence rather than promote its tested adaptation. A broad-universe daily news portfolio requires a separate declared contract and prospective input archive.

Each strategy needs its own portfolio state and opportunity schedule. A held Jev position must not remove another strategy's entry opportunities. Shared data and infrastructure expenses need a declared allocation. Incremental inference usage remains attributable to the strategy that requests it. A simulated portfolio does not create more broker capital.

The first observable deployed result is a report naming every strategy, its code and parameter identity, input coverage, positions, completed episodes, net execution P&L, applicable operating costs, and cost-stressed P&L. Missing costs and missing observations stay missing. Complete research input is a prerequisite for an economic verdict, not for honestly exposing the runner's current state.

Recent papers change the candidate priority. They do not replace the untouched evaluation that determines whether any candidate actually earns money in Bayn.
