# Wave 10D: orthogonal ETF Pillar 3

**DO NOT PROMOTE.** The single Holdout run scored all 34 preregistered trials, with no TIP proxy and no
reselections. None of the five frozen finalists qualified. No trial reached the 0.95 DSR threshold at any
cost, and no finalist improved the F4-only stack with a positive paired 95% lower bound at either allocation.

- **B/long/21 earned the strongest standalone evidence, not orthogonality.** At 10 bp it beat SHY by
  19.80 percentage points (7.43 after excluding the ten best excess days), with nominal bootstrap p = 0.03780.
  DSR was 0.77207 and Holdout F4 correlation was +0.6465. Nominal significance did not satisfy the multiple-trial gate.
- **C/-21/3/42 was the only frozen finalist that met turnover and correlation limits at all costs.** At 10 bp
  its Holdout excess was +2.23 percentage points but fell to −9.87 after the top-ten exclusion. Low correlation
  did not make the excess robust.
- **Some gains against the two-sleeve baseline did not carry over to F4 alone.** At 10 bp and 50% allocation,
  B lifted Holdout Sharpe by +0.7325 against F4+B0 (paired lower bound +0.0480), but only +0.0699 against F4
  (lower bound −0.3196). B0 itself lost 12.77% of compounded sleeve NAV at the extrapolated 20 bp round-trip cost.

This is a historical replay with modeled costs and sleeve transfers, not evidence of feasible fills, borrow availability,
or live broker, deployment, or capital authority. Holdout is unseen by this study's selection, not new to Bayn research.

## Holdout qualification

At 1% borrow, the gate requires positive Holdout excess over SHY before and after the top-ten exclusion,
nominal stationary-bootstrap p < 0.05, DSR ≥ 0.95, annual one-way turnover < 6× and absolute correlation
< 0.25 to both controls on Full and Holdout. At one common 25% or 50% weight across costs it also requires
positive paired lower bounds against both reference portfolios and positive Holdout Calmar lift against F4+B0.
Every condition is evaluated on unrounded values at 2, 5, **and** 10 bp one-way.

For an independent reproduction from a checkout with the committed receipt and the existing read-only

objects. Use the pinned Bun toolchain and write to new paths so the committed evidence is never overwritten:

: "${TIP_SOURCE:?Set TIP_SOURCE to the local supplement directory}"
runner=services/bayn/tools/research-10yr-orthogonal-pillar3.ts
