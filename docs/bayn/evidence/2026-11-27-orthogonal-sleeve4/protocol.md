## Rules fixed before holdout scoring

- Score each rule at all three stresses: 2, 5, 10 bp RT, 0.21 bp on modeled sale proceeds. Buy/sell slippage is adverse on both fills. Daily returns are additive net basis points on a fixed sleeve gross budget, not leveraged/compounded P&L. Missing input withholds the affected decision/leg; missing exact next-open print means unfilled IOC. A time exit remains working to the first observed open at or after its deadline, before the regular close. Never censor entries based on missing future exits: unresolved fills null the split economics. No post-close fallback.
- Development requires >0 net **and** >0 after removing the five best positive dates at both 5 and 10 bp, on each of Train and Validation, at least 20 traded dates, at least 80% decision coverage and at least 90% intended-leg fills. Require two or more profitable adjacent variants (and at least half of available neighbors): change one numeric axis by one grid step. Rank passing models first, then highest Validation 10 bp ex-top-five net, then identifier. Freeze exactly one model and its adjacent variants; if none passes, freeze the same ranking’s first rule only for negative-control diagnostics. Never replace it with a holdout winner.
- On Holdout require the same economic/sample gates and plateau, a centered circular five-session block bootstrap p < 0.05 (one-sided 10,000 draws), DSR ≥ 0.95, and positive ex-top-five after dropping each leg symbol where the portfolio admits that test. The DSR null is a zero-centered expected maximum over the total registered rule count (including live/daily and unavailable trials), using Validation cross-trial daily Sharpe dispersion with a unit-normal null SE floor. Costs are simultaneously required stresses, not independently selected hypotheses. SIP has a separate dispersion estimate. Live-only rules have no development period; their reported DSR borrows minute Validation dispersion and is descriptive, not a promotion test.
- Correlate day-aligned net modeled returns at each cost to the same frozen live tape replayed with the existing v2 verifier. Use only fully supported calendar dates for the reference and observed decisions with resolved candidate outcomes. Retain genuine flat days, reject undefined Pearson coefficients, and require ≥40 paired dates, ≥10 co-active dates per engine, and **absolute** correlation <0.25 to each engine at both 5 and 10 bp.
- All live-only and daily variants are diagnostics; record each as scored or untestable. Do not use their holdout scores to change the locked rule. Unselected core trials have no holdout score except the registered plateau neighbors. No production wiring or capital authority follows from a research pass.
