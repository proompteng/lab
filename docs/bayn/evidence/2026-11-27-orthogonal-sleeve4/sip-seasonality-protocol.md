# SIP follow-on: predeclared same-clock continuation screen

Test a different information set from the original same-day dispersion family. [Heston, Korajczyk and Sadka (2010)](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1540-6261.2010.01573.x) document cross-sectional continuation at the same half-hour on successive trading days. This is a small-universe adaptation of that hypothesis, not a direct replication or a claim of known institutional flow.

- **Panel:** use the new split-adjusted consolidated SIP minute tape. Trade the two fixed historical groups recorded in the tool: eight semiconductor/optical supply-chain names and five other Bayn single names. Exclude the seven names already observed across the original long sample and exclude SNDK because its standalone listing begins after Train starts. The present-day roster is retrospective, not point-in-time membership.
- **Signal:** rank each group by its mean observed open-to-open return in the same 30-minute exchange-local interval on the last 1, 5, 10 or 20 full trading sessions. Use all and only those preceding sessions; exclude early closes by the known exchange calendar. A group needs 80% history coverage. Equal-weight up to two long winners and short losers, with 50% of the sleeve gross budget available to each group. If its mean extreme-group spread does not exceed the registered 0 or 20 bp threshold, leave that group in cash.
- **Execution:** evaluate entry clocks 10:00, 12:30, 14:30 and 15:00 ET separately. The signal uses no current-session price, and entry at the clock is the next minute's observed open. Exit at the open 30 minutes later; if missing, retain the next available open before the scheduled close or mark the exit unresolved. Model 2/5/10 bp round-trip slippage and 0.21 bp on sales. Keep unfilled legs visible.
- **Selection:** evaluate exactly 64 rules on Train and Validation with the original chronological dates and the original economic, observation, fill and concentration gates. A plateau requires at least two and at least half of neighbors in the same clock interval to pass; a neighbor differs by one grid step in one of the lookback, tail size or spread dimensions. Rank qualifying rules by Validation 10 bp ex-top-five, then ID. Validation DSR includes the 264 original trials plus the 64 new ones, using the new cohort's cross-trial dispersion.
- **Holdout:** this development-only screen does not decode Apr–Nov 2026 prices or select an alternative from the original holdout. The previously inspected 2026 calendar cannot serve as a prospective holdout for a newly designed signal. If the new hypothesis qualifies in development, lock it and its neighbors before any retrospective replication, and require a fresh independent holdout for promotion. Benchmark-only SIP replay does not select any sleeve candidate.
