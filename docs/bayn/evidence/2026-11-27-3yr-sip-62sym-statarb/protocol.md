# Wave 2C: pre-registered SIP breadth and intraday reversal experiment

## What this test can establish

Compare sign-gated relative-VWAP breadth against median, sector ETF, from-open and ATR-normalized signals. Test whether deeper baskets improve both annualized daily Sharpe and net after removing the best days, not just total P&L. This is a historical, fixed-universe study, not a reconstruction of the investable universe or short availability on each date. The cohort is selected with hindsight. Neither a favorable result nor any pure strategy primitive authorizes trading, a v2 change, or a rollout.

The frozen 62-name manifest contains **46 individual stocks and 16 ETFs**, not 50 stocks and 12 ETFs. Its 50-name union of `bayn21` and `expanded29` includes IWM, QQQ, SMH and SPY. Bayn v2's 18-name Midday list includes IWM; excluding it leaves 17 stocks. Run both 17-vs-46 stock-only and exact Bayn-18/mixed-50 breadth controls; do not claim either is the requested 18-vs-50 **stock-only** result. Do not replace names or mix a different tape into this experiment. The nominal 2024–2026 dataset ends November 25, 2026.

## Identity, time and features

- Bind the supplied S3 manifest, gzip and calendar by SHA-256. Require 3,923,737 raw rows. Interpret the timestamp as the bar **start** in UTC, with New York exchange-calendar hours and DST. Archive rows outside the calendar's regular open (inclusive) and close (exclusive) are not RTH, even though the object's name says RTH; do not retime or backfill. Publish coverage and exclusions. Use the provider's split adjustment as delivered.
- Require 14 strictly earlier regular-way sessions with **all** valid RTH slots before an equity can be selected. Require current bars contiguous from the RTH open up through the decision; a missing bar cannot be filled forward in a signal or benchmark. Regular-way cutoffs: [ALAB 2024-03-20](https://ir.asteralabs.com/node/7756/ixbrl-viewer), [GEV 2024-04-02](https://www.gevernova.com/investors/faq), [SNDK 2025-02-24](https://investor.sandisk.com/static-files/edd4f57e-7fe6-486c-acf8-4bef3b0fdd62). The archive includes earlier SNDK prints; ignore them even for warmup. Reset WDC's warmup at the same distribution boundary; no position carries overnight.
- Session VWAP is volume-weighted SIP bar VWAP accumulated only through completed bars. Define deviation as `close / sessionVWAP - 1`. Use (1) deviation minus SPY deviation (v2-style signal control, not a replay of the live lifecycle), (2) deviation minus the current eligible cross-section median, (3) deviation minus the pre-assigned sector ETF deviation, (4) return from the session open minus the current eligible median from-open return, or (5) `(close - VWAP) / (priorATR14Ratio * sessionOpen)`. The sector residual is a **signal** adjustment, not a factor- or sector-exposure constraint on the book. The sector ETF lookup is fixed in the protocol source. ATR is the mean of prior valid daily true ranges divided by their respective session opens, using the last valid close for the gap term. Inverse-vol weights use sample standard deviation of the same 14 prior open-to-close returns (25 bp floor).
- Rank most negative for longs, most positive for shorts; alphabetically break ties. Use up to the configured number of pairs, equally many on each side and no one-sided fallback. Use no refills or later-bar substitutions after sending entries. Measure spread by the eligible VWAP deviation interquartile range (IQR; linear interpolation). Require IQR strictly larger than 35, 50 or 75 bp in the dispersion-gate ablations; for Nagel-inspired sizing multiply gross by `min(1, IQR / 75 bp)`. Moreira–Muir-inspired timing uses the previous 20 complete SPY open-to-close squared returns, scaling gross by `clamp(0.01² / meanSquaredReturn, 0.25, 1)`; no sizing before 20 such sessions. These are capped proxies, not literal replications of the papers.

## Registered grid and replay

- 17-stock and 18-name Bayn controls: SPY-relative, 2L+2S, each single entry clock. 50 mixed-name control: SPY-relative, 2–6 pairs at noon. No ETF orders in the 46-stock experiment; ETFs are reference series only.
- 46-stock grid: all five signals × 2, 3, 4, 5 or 6 pairs × 10:30, 11:00, 11:30, 12:00, 12:30 or 13:00 ET, equal dollars and 500 bp stop / 800 bp target. Noon one-factor ablations at depths 3–5 across all signals: 1.5×/2.5× ATR brackets, VWAP-cross exit with catastrophe stop, timed-only exit with catastrophe stop, inverse-vol sizing, dispersion gates at 35/50/75 bp, Nagel scaling and Moreira–Muir timing. The catastrophe stop is 1,500 bp and has no resting target.
- Two-wave ablation at depths 3–5 across all five signals, wide brackets and equal dollars. Use 10:30→13:30 and 12:00→15:55, plus shifting both **entries** by ±30 minutes. Reserve half the budget per wave; wave 2 excludes all wave-1 selections (even if not filled or already flat) and recomputes the remaining cross section. All timed exits are clipped to close − 5 min; skip a wave with no holding interval.
- A decision at 10:30 uses the 10:25 bar and earlier; the 10:30 open is the **next** bar's open. No same-bar close fills. Orders are independent (no atomic basket assumption), and a missing open can produce an orphan. VWAP-cross exits use completed bars then the next observed open, not the price of the cross. A timed flatten is at the scheduled open or the first observed subsequent RTH open; if none exists, the day/aggregate is unresolved, not zero. Never fabricate an intrabar execution in a gap; expose in-position missing bars separately.
- Stops gap adversely to the open; targets do not improve on a favorable gap. A known open event precedes the intrabar range; on ambiguous intrabar touches, stop wins. Pending market exits fill at the open before reading that bar's range, except an adverse stop gap takes precedence. If none triggers, flatten at the applicable clock. No overnight holdings, quote fills, skip-on-adverse-open rule or optimized entry collars.
- Constant $100,000 research equity, at most $180,000 gross allocation at entry, with dollars balanced by side and scaled down proportionally when fewer than the target pairs qualify. Inverse-vol weights normalize **within** side. This is an entry budget, not a cap on subsequent mark-to-market price drift. Use fractional reference notionals; long/short fills are stressed symmetrically by 1, 2.5 or 5 bp per side (2/5/10 bp RT) and 0.21 bp of actual sell notional. These are NBBO **slippage scenarios**, not quote measurements: SIP OHLCV contains no NBBO, queue, borrow, market-impact, auction participation, or locate evidence. Fees/borrow not explicitly modeled beyond the stated fee are research limitations.

## Stage boundary and metrics

1. Train: 2024-01-02–2025-06-30. Validation: 2025-07-01–2026-03-31. Holdout: 2026-04-01–2026-11-25. Report all six calendar half-years independently (including split boundaries inside 2026H1). Consecutive causal feature warmup may use earlier periods.
2. `--freeze` authenticates the whole compressed object but does not decode holdout prices, generate holdout trades, or compute holdout statistics. Rank the 46-stock trials within each signal family by Train Sharpe at 10 bp (then Train Ex-Top-5 at 10 bp, then trial ID). Eligible Train trials must pass the split and plateau gates below. Take at most the first from each family; Validation only vetoes a nominee, with no replacement. Record the nominees, approved holdout IDs, complete development economics, input/code identities and protocol in the receipt. Commit it before `--score`. Scoring requires that receipt and reproduces it from development data before reporting holdout. After scoring, failed trials cannot become nominees because of their holdout results.
3. Split gate: at **both 5 and 10 bp** require resolved positive net, positive Ex-Top-5 net and one-sided `BootP < 0.05`. Across all six half-years, require at least five with resolved positive net at **both** costs. Plateau: along at least one registered parameter axis (clock or depth, preserving other parameters), **both** immediately adjacent trials must have positive net and Ex-Top-5 and at least 75% of the focal Sharpe at both costs in each of Train, Validation and Holdout. Report all neighbor checks. No bootstrap or DSR threshold for neighbors. Boundary variants without a two-sided axis cannot qualify.
4. Include every calendar day in net, Ex-Top-5/10 and annualized sample Sharpe (zero for an observed no-trade day, not for unknown exits). Report execution diagnostics and gross/sell-fee/slippage totals. One-sided bootstrap of positive mean P&L: 10,000 mean-centered 5-session circular block replicates, add-one tail probability, fixed seed and calendar retained. Use the existing Bayn DSR implementation (Bailey–López de Prado approximation): estimate Sharpe dispersion from defined, resolved Train Sharpes; use the entire registered grid count as the multiple-testing penalty. Share that per-cost benchmark unchanged across subsequent periods. DSR is diagnostic, not substituted for the bootstrap. The research report does not interpret all-trials holdout diagnostics as independent discovery or a familywise significance claim.
5. LOSO means leave-one-**symbol**-out: rerun selection, residual center, dispersion, sizing and fills after removing each orderable symbol, leaving ETF reference series unchanged. Report per-omission net plus worst-period diagnostics; if executed P&L is unknown, preserve that unknown. This diagnoses concentration rather than providing a new validation cohort.
6. Only a pre-frozen nominee passing **every** gate merits a pure, tested v3-ready candidate. Do not change v2 policy, its hash, migrations 0222/0223, or runtime wiring. A winner on the 46-stock archive would still need separate evidence for the requested 50-stock comparison.
