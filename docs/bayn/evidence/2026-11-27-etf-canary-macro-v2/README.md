# Bayn Wave 4D: Ultra-low-turnover canary and macro ETF research

**No configuration qualified (0 of 135).** Twelve passed the structural test at all four costs:
nine ensembles and three standalone macro variants. Structural passes require positive net excess
over SHY and turnover below the cap in all three splits; they do not establish statistical robustness.

- **Canary and continuous TSMOM missed the turnover target.** Even the lowest-turnover variant in
  each family exceeded the 6×/yr cap in Holdout at every cost. No defensive sector comparator
  passed the structural test either.
- **Standalone macro beat the hurdle at low turnover in three variants.** These were
  `macro/63/42/0.1`, `macro/126/1/0.1`, and `macro/126/42/0.1`. None passed the strict split
  criteria at any cost, and none survived all 27 omissions. In particular, removing HYG or
  IEF prevents standalone regime classification; the rule then holds idle.
- **Only the pre-specified base ensemble cleared the strict split test at 2, 5, and 10 bp.**
  It went 0 of 8 on its own plateau at every cost. At 20 bp its Ex-Top-10 excess turned negative
  in Validation (−0.35 pp) and Holdout (−0.55 pp). Its Holdout p-value rose from 0.0478 at 2 bp
  to 0.0657 at 20 bp, and no configuration's Holdout cumulative DSR reached 0.95 (maximum 0.5670).
- **Treat this as a retrospective comparison, not independent validation.** Wave 3C had already
  inspected this tape. The 2 bp case models friction; adjusted daily SIP bars do not measure
  executable quotes, impact, or borrow availability. No result authorizes trading.

## Reading the complete evidence

Use `freeze.trials[i]` and `results.ids[i]` to identify each configuration.
`results.metrics[i][k][j]` contains two 14-metric books (SHY idle, then zero-yield idle), the
stationary-block bootstrap p-value, and cumulative DSR. The inference statistics use the
SHY-idle book's paired daily excess; DSR counts 257 trials cumulatively. Use
`scenarios[k]`, `windows[j]`, and `axes.books` for column labels. Each book's cumulative
excess is its net wealth minus frictionless SHY wealth, not the ratio of the two.
`loeo[s][i][k][j]` uses the symbol at `universe[s]` and Train, Validation, Holdout for `j`;
`axes.loeoMetrics` labels its fields. `plateau[i][k]` supplies exact passing and tested neighbor
counts, and `neighborsByTrial[i]` identifies the neighbors. The result's empty `qualified`
array records the decision across the full grid. If the JSON is too large for GitHub’s preview, view the raw file or read it from the checkout.
