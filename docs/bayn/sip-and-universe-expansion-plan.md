# Consolidated SIP and 73-symbol equity data plan

**Decision:** qualify a real-time SIP data lane in parallel with IEX before changing what any execution owner reads. Expand collection to 46 stocks and 27 ETFs, not trading eligibility. This is an engineering plan, not a rollout or a change to Bayn's PAPER authority, pinned v2 policy, order types, broker credentials, or Argo desired state.

The repository inventory below is relative to main at `bc7eb74af06eb399972493f8adf59aa7ff4f8073`. The November 27 readback independently found Torghut WS on `iex` with the same 22 names; archive on `iex` / `torghut-core-equity-v3`; the two Flink jobs running at parallelism 8 (TA) and 16 (archive); the existing raw topics at 3/13/3 partitions for bars/quotes/trades (RF 3); and ClickHouse at one shard/two replicas. Argo's live Bayn and Torghut Applications followed `kargo/bayn` and `kargo/torghut`, respectively, not main. These checks establish deployed topology, not headroom or SIP entitlement.

## Why separate data and execution changes

- The November 27 incident report identifies a 103-share GOOGL short at $271.0255 against a $271.08 signal reference (+2.01 bp adverse), a buy stop-limit at $275.31/$275.58 with a $275.88 mark, and 5,548 controller waits after the 13:00 ET regular close. A better quote source could improve evidence while the market is open; it cannot make an expired DAY order executable, guarantee a fill through a stop-limit collar, or make a closed-market quote fresh.
- In the repository's [microstructure evidence](evidence/2026-11-27-execution-microstructure/README.md) and [IEX RVOL study](evidence/2026-11-27-orb-zab-research/README.md), single-venue spreads on names such as LITE and COHR are large enough to distort execution checks. In [Wave 2D's retained results](https://github.com/proompteng/lab/blob/9b7d777035a139be81e22ec94b1ecfa6a882f921/docs/bayn/evidence/2026-11-27-3yr-sip-62sym-rvol-orb-etf/README.md), the OR5, 2.5× RVOL diagnostic totals about +$41.6k / +$35.3k at 5/10 bp across splits, but fails Ex-Top-5 in Validation and Holdout. [Wave 3C's ETF blend](https://github.com/proompteng/lab/blob/1731c7ede32f22306aaf55e101fc8354978b0445/docs/bayn/evidence/2026-11-27-10yr-multi-asset-risk-premia/README.md) totals +$53.8k across splits at 5 bp, but fails Holdout bootstrap at 10 bp and Ex-Top-5 at 20 bp. Neither study authorizes promotion. The ETF study uses daily, next-open execution; its results do not qualify minute-level trading or quote coverage.
- Keep three separate acceptance decisions: **capture quality and capacity**, **shadow parity and replay**, then **a reviewed policy and execution-safety decision**. Do not spend a strategy's apparent edge by widening freshness or stop limits to make the data lane pass.

## Capacity proof before adoption

**Provider.** Alpaca documents Basic as 30 equity WebSocket symbols and Algo Trader Plus as unlimited with real-time coverage; historical limits are 200 vs 10,000 requests/minute. Provider symbol count is not Dorvud's symbol-channel counter. The stream may allow only one connection per entitlement/endpoint subscription; verify the actual credential and concurrent ownership rather than scaling replicas. Inspect authenticated subscription acknowledgements for the exact symbol set and required channels; fail on 405 (symbol limit), 406 (connection limit), slow-client disconnects, 429, or delayed/denied feed. A successful historical query, especially for an older window, is not real-time entitlement. [Alpaca plans](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/about-market-data-api), [stream errors and connection limits](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/streaming-market-data), [equity feed URLs](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/real-time-stock-pricing-data), [explicit REST feed parameter](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/reference/stocklatestquotes-1).

**Read-only baseline, 15:00–16:00Z November 27.** Mimir returned about 3.44 million Torghut WS Kafka send observations (~955/s across producer routes), with estimated p99 acknowledgement latency 38 ms. At 16:00Z the last Flink checkpoint took 4.20 s (TA) and 3.26 s (archive); max task backpressure over the hour was 0 and 222 ms/s respectively. The Kafka consumer max record lag peaked at 0 and 2,050 respectively. ClickHouse reported about 6.31 million inserted rows across its scraped replicas over the hour (all tables/workloads, not a distinct equity row count). No Torghut ClickHouse PVC-available series was returned; this does not establish disk headroom.

The [Bayn offset and coverage log](../../services/bayn/src/market-data/streaming/kafka.ts#L777-L813) in Loki gives a partition-level cross-check: between 15:59:29 and 15:59:59Z, quote partition 9 advanced ~143 offsets/s (more than half the sampled quote offsets), versus ~81/s on partition 1; nine of the 13 partitions advanced by under one offset/s. Peak sampled trade-partition rate was ~5 offsets/s. At the second sample the raw-topic offsets were caught up, the queue was empty, and SPY/GOOGL had 30/30 bars and matched features, but quote ages were 58/2,691 ms respectively. At 20:31:39Z the projection still reported available, with GOOGL quote age **9,101,347 ms**. These samples show skew and the difference between transport availability and usable data. They are not a SIP throughput forecast or a feature-to-projection latency distribution. No authenticated 73-name SIP traffic was generated.

## Running the forensic report

Use the existing read-only Postgres URL and flag order; see the [alpha cutover runbook](intraday-alpha-cutover.md#wave-1d-l1-replay-and-post-session-audit). Legacy fields remain keyed to the supplied `--regular-close`. The added `forensics` section prefers a consistent verified provider regular close, lists each compared execution close and discloses CLI fallback, conflict or mismatch.

`entryOrders` gives canonical fill VWAP and signed cost vs the target plan, signal bar and each order's limit; positive adverse bps is worse, positive limit improvement is better. Partial/missed counts are **planned legs**, including attempted legs that never obtained an order. `exitOrders` uses the signed inventory episode, not time proximity; the original entry bar is a price-movement anchor, while a verified close plan reference or native child trigger is the separate exit-execution benchmark. Unknown references stay null. The exact VWAP ratio is in nanodollar atoms; decimal bps is for display. Broker fill totals are shown separately when retained fill events are incomplete.

`stopLimits` identifies expired/canceled unfilled protections with same-side-held broker marks between entry fill and termination (or earlier inventory exit). A breach beyond the stop-limit collar is evidence of a **possible** gap/deadlock, not proof of an eligible trigger print or executable liquidity; absence of a sampled breach is not proof the stop worked. `atCloseInventory` is the signed ledger at regular close; `postCloseNonzeroPositions` retains the first later nonzero broker observation per symbol, even if it was subsequently closed. `strandedPositions` lists later broker exposures corroborated by the same-side ledger at regular close. If one side of that evidence is missing, investigate instead of treating an empty corroborated list as flat. Tick counts are distinct retained controller epoch/sequence completions for the account's session date; no retained ticks yields null, not zero.

The daily breakeven verdict compares **2 × reference-notional-weighted realized entry bps** to the [Wave 2B v2](evidence/2026-11-27-3yr-sip-bayn21-v2/README.md) 6.00 bp and supplied v3 21.92 bp round-trip reference thresholds. PASS is below both on complete signal-bar entry evidence after close; WARN covers missing/incomplete evidence, equality, or failing v2 alone; FAIL means the observed proxy exceeds both. A target-reference fallback is disclosed and cannot establish a PASS. This is neither measured exit cost nor all-in P&L (fees, borrow, missed fills and stranded positions are separate); a low entry cost can coexist with an execution incident.
